“a quiet thank you, kept like a voicemail”

on this device. nothing has left yet.

to amma, late at night

october 24 • quiet room

I made the rasam exactly the way you told me on that Sunday morning when the kitchen was full of steam and rain. I still have the voicemail from two winters ago saved on my phone. Sometimes I play the first four seconds just to remember the rhythm of your voice calling my name.

I didn't say it enough when you were sitting right across the table from me, but here in this quiet house, I wanted to say a simple thank you for how safe you made the whole world feel.

goodnight amma. sleep deeply.
