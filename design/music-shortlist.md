- [Ambient Piano by FreeMusicForVideo](https://pixabay.com/music/solo-piano-ambient-piano-524039/): 2:06, Content ID registered, tagged hopeful and bright; kept off the list for being the shortest and brightest.
- [Atrium (Neoclassical Felt Piano) by KonstantinPazuzuStudio](https://pixabay.com/music/solo-piano-atrium-neoclassical-felt-piano-501495/): tagged morning, sunshine, sunrise, bright; wrong time of day.
- [Soft Aura Pad](https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-soft-aura-pad-short-texture-450952/), [soft airy ambient pad warm angelic](https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-soft-airy-ambient-pad-warm-angelic-93-449672/) and [Calm Ambient Dreamscape by morgan-ambient](https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-calm-ambient-dreamscape-529861/): Pixabay licence is fine, but all three are marked AI generated, and the Dreamscape is also tagged beats, upbeat and future bass. - Pixabay music box tracks by Universfield and leberch ("Horror Music Box" and similar): listing only, not opened; horror and dark mystery mood.
- Debussy "Clair de Lune" and "Arabesque" music box versions by GregorQuendel, plus Gymnopedie No. 1 and Canon in D by KonstantinPazuzuStudio: listing only, not opened; famous melodies are exactly the hummable tune we want to avoid.
- "Nothing to Lose" by StarbackMusic (sad celesta and piano): listing only, not opened; 6:28 is over the length limit.
- Freesound dark drones (Void Monolith, Dark Pad Synth, Sci-fi Ambient Drone and similar): CC0 but dark, heavy or sci-fi; wrong mood.
- Anything outside Pixabay Content License or CC0 (CC BY, CC BY-NC, YouTube Audio Library, BBC): not looked at, per the licence rule.
